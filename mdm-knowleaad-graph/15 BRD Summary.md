# 15 BRD Summary

## Source

- `BRD.md`

## Business Goal

The project is intended to be a **Unified Data Platform** for event organizations, focused on centralizing customer and operational data.

## Main Business Objectives

- centralize customer data
- improve CRM efficiency
- improve collaboration and task management
- provide real-time insight
- improve security and compliance
- reduce manual data entry and processing time

## Business-Critical Capability Areas

- customer and data management
- assignment/task management
- dynamic data models
- import/export
- dashboards and analytics
- user management and security

## Business Success Metrics Mentioned

- 50% reduction in customer-data management effort
- 90% adoption within 6 months
- 99.9% uptime
- high data accuracy
- compliance support
