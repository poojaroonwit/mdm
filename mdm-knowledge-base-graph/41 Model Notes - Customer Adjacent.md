# 41 Model Notes - Customer Adjacent

## Important Reality Check

The repository language still references customers heavily, but the modern platform scope is wider than a classic CRM. Treat “customer” as one important business surface, not the whole product identity.

## Model Adjacency Areas

- customers and companies routes
- structured data models
- EAV entities
- assignments / tickets / projects
- attachments and activity logs

## Why This Note Exists

The codebase spans:

- business-facing customer management flows
- generic platform data-modeling capabilities

That dual identity is a major source of both product flexibility and documentation drift.
